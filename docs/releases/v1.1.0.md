# v1.1.0 — Swing Zones

Released 2026-11-08.

## Added

- OTE band at logarithmic ratios 0.618–0.786, with an optional 0.700 or 0.705 reference.
- Subtle Premium and Discount regions around the working swing's geometric midpoint.
- Equilibrium: a dedicated dashed 0.5 line and price label.
- Independent switches, theme-aware Auto colors and Custom fill/color settings.

## Display behavior

Zones follow the working swing. OTE appears in Discount for UP swings and Premium for DOWN swings. Only current zones are displayed; no historical zone archive is created. Matching classic Fibonacci annotations are temporarily omitted while dedicated EQ/OTE annotations are visible. Zones do not require the classic Fibonacci layer to be enabled.

## Preserved

Structural break rules, Cement completion, swing construction and FVG maintenance are unchanged. Open-bar results remain provisional. This update adds a visualization layer, not a new market-structure algorithm or a trading signal.

See the [Swing Zones guide](../SWING_ZONES.md) for settings and examples.
