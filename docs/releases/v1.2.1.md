# v1.2.1 — FVG Display Fix

Released 2026-11-08.

## Fixed

Overlapping or touching FVG fragments in the same direction are drawn as one region. Repeated coverage no longer darkens the fill.

Positive price gaps remain separate. Bullish and bearish regions retain their own colors and remain separate, including where opposite-direction regions overlap.

## Preserved

This is a display-only change. Stored FVG records, gap detection, trimming/splitting and fill handling are unchanged. The original full-fragment eligibility filter and reference-swing logarithmic display range are retained, as is the current-bar to 30-bars-ahead horizontal extent.

MST structural rules, break tolerance, Cement and logarithmic Fibonacci/Swing Zones calculations are unchanged. No settings are added or changed.
