# v1.2.2 — Drawing Reuse

Released 2026-11-08.

## Changed

- Reuse the two working-swing boundary arrows, updating coordinates and color instead of deleting and recreating them each update.
- Reuse classic Fibonacci line/label pairs, updating level positions, text and colors. Remove surplus pairs when fewer levels are visible.
- Reuse FVG display boxes, updating coordinates and colors. Create boxes when needed and remove surplus boxes when the displayed region count falls.
- Recreate drawing handles removed by platform garbage collection.

## Preserved

MST swing formation, BOS/CHoCH, break tolerance and Cement rules are unchanged. Fibonacci and Swing Zones retain their logarithmic calculations, level orientation and annotation rules.

FVG detection, stored-fragment maintenance, same-direction display union and the logarithmic visibility range are unchanged. Displayed boxes retain their current-bar to 30-bars-ahead extent. No age/count filter or new setting is introduced.

This update reduces drawing creation/deletion; it does not cap stored FVG history or claim a measured speed increase. Open-bar behavior remains provisional.
